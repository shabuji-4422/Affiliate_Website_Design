Trending doesn't always mean good. Before a product appears on TrendFinds, it has to pass every one of these checks.

### Where we find trends

We check Amazon's **Best Sellers**, **Movers & Shakers**, **Hot New Releases** and **Most Wished For** lists several times a week, and we watch what's going viral on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. A product that shows up in more than one place gets a closer look.

### What a product must pass

- **Strong ratings:** usually 4.3 stars or higher, from a large number of buyers
- **Recent reviews still positive:** we read the last one to two months of reviews, not just the overall score
- **Reliable seller:** sold or shipped by Amazon or a well-rated seller
- **Low return risk:** no pattern of "broke after a week" or sizing complaints
- **Real usefulness:** we can explain who it's for and what problem it solves

### Keeping things up to date

Products sell out, get worse, or get replaced by better options. We review our guides regularly and swap out anything that no longer passes these checks. Prices on Amazon change often, so we don't list exact prices; always check the current price on Amazon before you buy.

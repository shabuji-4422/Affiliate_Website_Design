This Privacy Policy explains what information TrendFinds ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website and how it is used.

### Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, address or payment details. Our hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working.

If you subscribe to our email list, we collect your email address only to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

### Cookies and affiliate links

This site contains links to Amazon.com. When you click one, Amazon may set cookies in your browser so that it can identify that you came from our site. Those cookies are controlled by Amazon and covered by [Amazon's Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy).

If we add analytics in the future, we will use it only to understand which pages are useful, and we will update this policy.

### Third-party websites

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites, including Amazon. Please review their policies when you leave our site.

### Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
